= Coverage and gaps

Interscript ships a registry of authority-backed transliteration
systems. This page states what is covered today, what is not, and how
the gap list is tracked. Every claim here links to a source you can
check.

== What is covered

The map registry contains **318 systems** drawn from
https://github.com/interscript/maps[the maps repository] — each an
executable, test-carrying transliteration map with a named authority
and source document. Browse them on the
https://interscript.org/maps[map catalogue]; every system has its own
page with its description, source document, scripts, mapping counts,
invertibility class, a live in-browser preview, and its full rule
chart.

Invertibility is measured per system and recorded in the
https://github.com/interscript/maps/tree/main/docs/coverage[coverage
ledger]: systems are classed as lossless one-to-one (reversible),
lossy many-to-one, or context-only. The counts are re-derived from the
maps themselves, not asserted.

== What is not covered yet

The open queue is tracked as work orders in
https://github.com/interscript/interscript-models/tree/main/TODO.sota[the
models repository's campaign ledger]. The remaining systems, by
source:

* *DIN* — 1460-2 (Cyrillic, part 2), 31635 (Persian, Kurdish and Urdu
  columns; the Arabic column is shipped), 31636 (Yiddish column; the
  Hebrew column is shipped), 32708.
* *BSI PD 6505:1982* — the romanization of Korean (a mechanical BSI
  system, not McCune-Reischauer); transcription from the scan is in
  progress.
* *ISO* — ISO 9 non-Russian Cyrillic coverage check, ISO 259 Hebrew
  letter table, ISO 7089/7098 Chinese pinyin, and the remaining ISO
  15919 Indic scripts.
* *ALA-LC* — the remaining systems of the ALA-LC romanization guide
  beyond the 55 ALA-LC maps already in the registry, queued in
  batches.
* *ICAO and UNGEGN* — current source documents need to be diffed
  against the registry.

== Neural lines with open targets

The neural (learned) model lines and their open quality targets are
tracked in the same ledger. Where a line's next step depends on a
license or a dataset only the project owner can grant — additional
Hebrew vocalization data, commercial-license Urdu dictionaries, the
SEAlang and full Chuon Nath sources for Khmer — that gate is recorded
as owner-held and the line waits.

== How to check any of this

* Registry source: https://github.com/interscript/maps[maps repo]
* Coverage ledger:
  https://github.com/interscript/maps/tree/main/docs/coverage[maps/docs/coverage]
* Campaign ledger (what is open, what shipped, what is blocked):
  https://github.com/interscript/interscript-models/tree/main/TODO.sota[interscript-models/TODO.sota]
