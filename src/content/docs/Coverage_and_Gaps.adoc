= Coverage and gaps

Interscript ships a registry of authority-backed transliteration
systems. This page states what is covered today, what is not, and how
the gap list is tracked. Every claim here links to a source you can
check.

== What is covered

The map registry contains **457 systems** drawn from
https://github.com/interscript/maps[the maps repository] — each an
executable, test-carrying transliteration map with a named authority
and source document, 16,325 embedded tests from those documents, all
green. Browse them on the
https://interscript.org/maps[map catalogue]; every system has its own
page with its description, source document, scripts, mapping counts,
invertibility class, a live in-browser preview, and its full rule
chart.

Invertibility is measured per system and recorded in the
https://github.com/interscript/maps/tree/main/docs/coverage[coverage
ledger]: systems are classed as lossless one-to-one (reversible),
lossy many-to-one, or context-only. The counts are re-derived from the
maps themselves, not asserted.

The six ISO 2026-edition systems added in maps v3.3.2 follow the
provenance conventions of the
https://github.com/interscript/maps/tree/main/docs/coverage[coverage
ledger]. Three are encoded from purchased published editions —
verification-grade sources acquired from the ISO Store (order
OP-1130631): `iso-kat-Geor-Latn-9984-2026` (ISO 9984:2026, second
edition), `iso-hye-Armn-Latn-9985-2026` (ISO 9985:2026, second
edition; no first-edition map exists), and `iso-prs-Arab-Latn-233-3-2026`
(ISO 233-3:2026, third edition, the strict clause-4 system) — each
carrying exhaustive per-row embedded tests generated from its
row-sources provenance artifact. Three are encoded from FDIS ballot
copies held in the project's private reference collection and await
verification against the published editions: `iso-rus-Cyrl-Latn-9-2026`
(ISO/FDIS 9:2026, ballot N127) and the ISO/FDIS 233-1:2026 Arabic pair
`iso-ara-Arab-Latn-233-2026` and `iso-ara-Arab-Latn-233-2026-modified`
(ballot N129; the strict clause-4 system and the clause-5 modified
system, which share the `ara-latn-233-2026` ruleset library). The
in-place refreshes of `iso-tha-Thai-Latn-11940-1998` and the Pali map
that runs through it correct the phinthu rendering (the spurious en
dash before ring below is gone) under the interim working source
recorded in the coverage ledger.

== What is not covered yet

The open queue is tracked as work orders in
https://github.com/interscript/interscript-models/tree/main/TODO.sota[the
models repository's campaign ledger]. The remaining systems, by
source:

* *ISO* — ISO 9 non-Russian Cyrillic coverage check, ISO 7089/7098
  Chinese pinyin, and the remaining ISO 15919 Indic scripts. The source
  documents are not yet in the project's reference collection;
  acquisition is owner-held. The published editions that would lift the
  FDIS-grade maps (ISO 9:2026, ISO 233-1:2026) to published-edition
  provenance are likewise owner-held acquisitions.
* *ALA-LC* — the batch campaigns landed through 2026-11-09 (Brahmic/
  SE-Asian, Arabic-script, Cyrillic, East Asian, and misc-script
  systems; 93 ALA-LC maps in the registry). The systems listed in the
  campaign ledger without a source document in the collection —
  moghol, bashkir, chuvash, udmurt, komi, yakut/sakha, N'Ko,
  tod-oirat — are recorded there, not encodable until a source is
  acquired.
* *ICAO and UNGEGN* — the source-document diff against the registry
  is complete (2026-11-08), and the UNGEGN editions are acquired into
  the reference library.

== Neural lines with open targets

The neural (learned) model lines and their open quality targets are
tracked in the same ledger. Where a line's next step depends on a
license or a dataset only the project owner can grant — additional
Hebrew vocalization data, commercial-license Urdu dictionaries, the
SEAlang and full Chuon Nath sources for Khmer — that gate is recorded
as owner-held and the line waits.
